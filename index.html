Reading the Earth through radar.

I'm Simon Dagbanja, a remote sensing researcher. I build label-efficient, physics-informed methods for SAR and InSAR, aimed at environmental monitoring in data-scarce regions.

My InSAR work replaces the hand-designed cost in network-flow phase unwrapping with a learned prior that preserves the solver's zero-residue guarantee, validated on coseismic interferograms against GPS. In few-shot SAR, I design frozen-backbone, physics-calibrated methods that map floods from only a handful of labelled examples.

I'm currently a graduate student at UESTC under Prof. Qian Jiang, with first-author manuscripts accepted at IEEE AP-GARSS 2026 and under review at IEEE TGRS. Before radar, I trained as a geographer at the University of Ghana, with field, UAV, and GIS work across Ghana.

2026Manuscript · Submitted for review at IEEE AP-GARSS 2026
InSARPhase UnwrappingSwin + LoRA

Learned Statistical Costs for Network-Flow InSAR Phase Unwrapping

Rather than replacing the minimum-cost-flow solver, I learn its cost function: a Swin Transformer with low-rank adaptation predicts per-edge ambiguity probabilities, fused with a coherence term and made differentiable through the solver. It lowers unwrapping error by over 48% versus SNAPHU, stays residue-free on Sentinel-1 coseismic interferograms, and agrees with GPS to approximately 2 cm, with no retraining on real data. Validated on Ridgecrest, Kumamoto, Amatrice, and Gorkha events.

2026Manuscript · Under review at IEEE TGRS
InSARCross-BandL-Band

Cross-Band Learned Statistical Costs for Network-Flow InSAR Phase Unwrapping

Extending the learned-cost unwrapper into a full cross-band framework: a first-principles L-band phase-noise model, LoRA-based C→L transfer with a formal forgetting bound, and a five-arc minimum-cost-flow that widens the ambiguity range for near-fault and volcanic scenes. Validated across Sentinel-1 and NISAR against independent GPS.

2026Manuscript · Under review at IEEE TGRS
InSARL-BandBenchmark

LB-DLPU: A L-Band Benchmark Dataset for Deep-Learning InSAR Phase Unwrapping

Created LB-DLPU, a comprehensive L-band phase unwrapping benchmark for deep learning with a 13-method baseline study. Provides standardized training and evaluation protocols to advance L-band InSAR processing research.

2026Manuscript · In preparation
Few-ShotSARFlood Mapping

TopoProto: Physics-Informed Few-Shot SAR Flood Segmentation

Integrating HAND (Height Above Nearest Drainage) and slope priors into a prototype-based few-shot segmentation framework for SAR flood mapping. Targets Remote Sensing of Environment.
